# Karma Compass

Karma Compass helps nonprofit board members prepare meetings and work with
their organization's agendas, minutes, decisions, actions and documents.
Connect the Karma Compass connector from this plugin's Connectors tab and
authorize your existing Compass account. Your membership, permissions and
plan determine the records and actions available. The connector does not
transfer money, buy travel or file official tax returns. Human approval
remains required for official publication and other protected decisions.

Try asking: "Help me prepare our next board meeting using our current agenda
and previous minutes." The assistant distinguishes returned records, missing
sources and draft suggestions. It reports saved changes only after checking
the returned record. A Compass account and board membership are required.
