# Work with board records through the connector

Discover each operation and inspect its contract through MCP. The following
procedures carry the platform's governance and meeting-record rules into this
host; they do not grant additional tools or authority.

## Answer from documents and minutes

Search citations before answering a factual question about bylaws, policies,
packets or what a meeting discussed. Inspect the passages and their source
versions. When the catalog supports recording citations, use its contract for
the exact returned text; cite readable document titles, pages and links.
Label draft minutes as awaiting adoption. A meeting manager's save of the
reviewed draft starts its extracted action items before adoption; use action
records to check their status. An empty search or a metadata
summary does not prove that minutes or a document do not exist. If semantic
search is unavailable, explain that only text search was available.

## Prepare or revise minutes

Find the meeting and its saved source. For a new upload, discover the supported
upload contract, collect its required rights or consent attestation and obey
the returned size and content limits. A chat attachment is not a Compass
upload until the API returns a receipt. If this host cannot access its bytes,
offer pasted notes or the platform's upload screen. Keep credentials and storage
references out of the conversation.

Read source status before asking the API to prepare minutes. Treat queued work
as pending. Read the review list and the selected review's source and current
version; do not assume a draft list without records means there are no drafts.
Prepare a cited draft or revise the selected review through the discovered
operations. Do not substitute the internal unattended extraction tools.

Read back the saved review. Adoption needs the person's explicit action in
Compass: request it when asked and return its review link. Emailing draft or
adopted minutes is a separate request with message and recipient confirmation.
Read the relevant delivery receipt; sending a draft does not prove the adopted
version was sent. A queued or ambiguous delivery is not confirmed delivery.

## Follow up on action items

Find an action by its title in the full action list, not just a capped dashboard.
Apply a requested date window to the source meeting date, or creation date for
an item without a meeting. List prepared or older lines that have not started
separately from open or overdue actions.

For reminders, read Mission Control and recent contact status. Explain any
paused email setting or plan restriction. Avoid duplicate contact when an
earlier message is sent, queued or has an uncertain outcome. Prepare a follow-up
when permitted. Show the draft and recipients before sending, and report the
API's receipt rather than assuming an email arrived.

## Decisions, votes, people and playbooks

Find the current record before changing it. A discussion is not a vote, a draft
motion is not open voting and a closed ballot is not certification. Ask for
voting rules the person attests from their bylaws; do not supply guessed rules.
Use the operation's confirmation rules for votes, deletions, legal holds and
organization email settings. Hand membership invitations and withdrawals to
the person using the returned Compass link.

For playbooks, discover the review and preview steps before activation. Obtain
the member's review and the required preview for anything that emails people.
Use only execution modes the connector permits. Report an unavailable action
with the API's recovery instruction; an internal agent's broader toolset is
not an alternative authorization path.
